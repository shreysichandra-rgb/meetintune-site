Your privacy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

InTune is your private helper on WhatsApp. It remembers what you tell it, gives it back when you ask, and sends the reminders you ask for. Here is, in plain words, what it keeps and why. InTune does not give medical advice.

Who runs InTune

InTune is run by Shreysi Chandra. For any privacy question, write to shreysi.chandra@gmail.com.

What InTune remembers

InTune keeps what you tell it, so that it can remember it for you. Share only what you'd like it to remember. It keeps:

  • Your phone number, your WhatsApp name (and your name, if you share it), your language, and the date your free days end.
  • The things you tell it: symptoms and periods, medicines and diet plans as you report them, doctor questions, appointments, to-dos and your grocery list.
  • The reminders you ask for.
  • Your conversation with InTune, kept securely so it can follow along, and so we can put things right if something goes wrong.
  • Small technical labels from WhatsApp, so a message is never answered twice.

InTune can read voice notes, photos and PDFs. It reads each one once to keep what's in it, such as your diet plan. The recording or file itself is not kept.

Why

Only to help you: to remember what you share, answer you, send your reminders, and make your doctor page when you ask.

The services InTune uses

  • WhatsApp carries your messages.
  • Convex stores InTune's information securely.
  • OpenAI helps InTune understand your messages and voice notes. It sees your message and the notes it needs to answer you, never your phone number.
  • Razorpay handles payment if you choose to pay. Your card and bank details never reach InTune.
  • GitHub hosts this website. It has nothing from your chats.

Each of these follows its own privacy terms.

You are in control

Your notes stay until you delete them. Send Delete my data any time to remove everything, or ask InTune to remove just one note. Sending Stop stops InTune and keeps your notes; Pause quiets reminders. More on deleting your data.
