Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

InTune is a WhatsApp helper for women in perimenopause. It remembers what you tell it, gives it back when you ask, and sends the reminders you ask for. It does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment advice.

Who operates InTune

InTune is operated by Shreysi Chandra. For privacy questions, contact shreysi.chandra@gmail.com.

What we keep

Using InTune means it keeps what you send it, so that it can remember it for you. Your messages may contain sensitive health information, so please send only what you want InTune to keep. We store:

  • Your phone number, WhatsApp profile name, your name if you give it, your language, whether InTune is active, paused or stopped, whether you are on the waitlist or let in, and when your free days end.
  • What InTune saves from your messages: notes, events, plans, medicines and food plans as you report them, grocery items, doctor questions, appointments and to-dos, and what you mark done or skipped.
  • The reminders you ask for.
  • Your messages to InTune and its replies, with their times and technical details of how each was processed. InTune's small team can privately review these to find and fix problems.
  • WhatsApp message identifiers, used so the same message is not answered twice.

InTune reads typed messages, voice notes, photos and PDFs. A voice note is turned into text once and handled like a typed message; a photo or PDF is read once to keep what is in it. The audio and files themselves are not kept. Videos are not read.

Why we use it

To understand your messages, keep and correct your records, answer you, send your reminders, make your doctor page when you ask, and investigate problems.

Service providers

  • Meta/WhatsApp carries your messages and our replies, including your phone number.
  • Convex hosts InTune's backend and database.
  • OpenAI interprets your messages and turns voice notes into text. Each request includes your message (and any voice note, photo or PDF), your saved records and reminders, your recent conversation, and your name if you gave it. It does not include your phone number.
  • Razorpay handles payment if you choose to pay. We keep order and payment references, never your card or bank details.
  • GitHub hosts this public website. It receives no personal information from your InTune chats.

Their processing and retention are governed by their own terms.

How long we keep it, and how to delete it

Your information stays until you delete it. To delete everything, send Delete my data. You can also ask InTune to remove one particular note. See data deletion for exactly what is removed and what remains. Sending Stop stops InTune but keeps your records. Sending Pause quiets reminders only.
