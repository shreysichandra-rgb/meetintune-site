What InTune is

InTune is a helper on WhatsApp for women in perimenopause. It remembers what you tell it, gives it back when you ask, sends reminders you ask for, and can make a summary page for your doctor. It reads typed messages, voice notes, photos and PDFs and replies in text.

InTune is not a medical service. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice, and it is not a substitute for a doctor. Always follow your doctor's advice. In an emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital.

Who can use it

You must be 18 or older and use your own WhatsApp number. New users may be placed on a waitlist and let in by the InTune team.
