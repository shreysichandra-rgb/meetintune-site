InTune Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 5 November 2026

InTune is operated by Shreysi Chandra. For deletion questions, contact shreysi.chandra@gmail.com.

Delete your information

Open your WhatsApp conversation with InTune, from the number you used, and send exactly:

Delete my data

Look for this confirmation:

Done. Everything you told me has been deleted. Your number is all that remains, so that I can recognise you if you write again.

If you do not get this reply, contact shreysi.chandra@gmail.com. Do not assume deletion succeeded.

What is deleted

Everything InTune saved from your messages, what you marked done or skipped, your reminders, and your stored messages and InTune’s replies, including their technical details.

What remains

  • Your phone number and account entry, so InTune can recognise you if you write again.
  • WhatsApp message identifiers, which contain no message text.
  • Records of any payment you made: order and payment references, dates and your paid-until date.

Deletion does not remove messages from your own WhatsApp chat, or guarantee removal from service providers’ logs or backups. Private exports made before you deleted are not covered automatically; contact us if this concerns you.

Stop is different

Sending Stop stops InTune but keeps your records. If you write to InTune after deleting, it starts fresh.
