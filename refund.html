Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Cancellation

There is nothing to cancel. Each payment covers 30 days and does not renew automatically. If you don't pay again, your paid days simply end. You can also send "Stop" to InTune at any time.

Refunds

If InTune isn't working for you, message us within 7 days of paying and we will refund your payment in full. After 7 days, payments are not refundable, but your paid days continue until they end.

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay and usually reach you within 5 to 7 working days.

Failed or double payments

If money was taken but your access did not start, or you were charged twice, send "I paid" to InTune or email us. We will fix it or refund the extra payment.

Delivery

InTune is a digital service on WhatsApp. Nothing is shipped. Your paid days start as soon as the payment is confirmed.
