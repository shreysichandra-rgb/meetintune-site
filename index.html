A helper, not a doctor

InTune remembers and reminds. It doesn't diagnose, say what's normal, or advise on medicines. Those questions are saved for your doctor. Your chat is private and one-to-one, never a group, and "Delete my data" removes everything. In an emergency, call 112.

InTune is free while we're in a private beta. If that ever changes, we'll tell you first, and nothing is charged without your say.

No. InTune isn't a doctor and never gives medical advice. It keeps your notes and questions so you can get the most from your doctor.
